# Licensing

Insight UI is available under two licences. You choose which one applies to you.

## Open source — AGPL-3.0

The code in this repository is licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). You may use, study, modify
and redistribute it under those terms.

The AGPL obligation that surprises people most often: section 13 extends
copyleft to network use. If you modify Insight UI and let users interact with
it over a network, you must offer those users the corresponding source of your
modified version — not only when you distribute a binary.

For most contributors, internal projects and other AGPL software, this is the
licence you want and there is nothing further to do.

## Commercial — Insight UI Enterprise

If the terms of the AGPL-3.0 do not meet your needs, a commercial licence is
available. It may be suitable if you:

- want to integrate Insight UI into proprietary products
- do not want to disclose your source code modifications
- need to distribute the software without AGPL-3.0 obligations
- require additional support, warranty, or indemnification terms

Alongside the removal of the copyleft obligation, the Enterprise licence covers:

- commercial support with agreed response times
- Enterprise-only components and features
- AI-assisted features
- release channels with a longer maintenance window

## Why contributions need a CLA

Offering both licences only works if Alpin Insight Solutions holds the rights to
license every part of the code under both. A contribution that arrives under the
AGPL alone could not be included in an Enterprise release — and could not
easily be removed later either, once it is woven into the codebase.

The [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md) resolves this. You keep the
copyright in your contribution and may use it however you like; you grant us the
additional right to license it commercially, and we commit in return to keep
licensing it under the open source licence as well.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how signing works in practice.
